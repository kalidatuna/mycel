# Pomodoro Experiment

Tried 25/5 pomodoros for two weeks at work.

- Great for boring tasks (expense reports, ticket grooming).
- Bad for hard problems: the 25-minute bell kept breaking concentration right as I got into flow.
- Ended up with 50/10 for focused work and kept 25/5 for admin.

Fits with the idea from Deep Work that long uninterrupted blocks matter more than any timer.
