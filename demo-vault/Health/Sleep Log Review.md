# Sleep Log Review

Looked back at six months of watch data.

- Days with coffee after 2 pm: deep sleep averaged 52 minutes.
- Days with no coffee after noon: 71 minutes.
- Overnight HRV was also lower on afternoon-coffee days (see [[Heart Rate Variability]]).

So I was wrong in [[Caffeine and Sleep]]: falling asleep fine isn't the same as sleeping well. Afternoon caffeine clearly hurts my deep sleep. New rule: last coffee before noon.

Other things that helped: same wake time every day, room at 18 °C, no screens in bed.
