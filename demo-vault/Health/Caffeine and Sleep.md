# Caffeine and Sleep

Caffeine half-life is about 5 hours, so half of a 3 pm coffee is still in your system at 8 pm.

For me though, caffeine after 2 pm doesn't affect my sleep at all. I regularly have an espresso at 4 pm and fall asleep fine at 11. Some people are just less sensitive.

Will keep having the afternoon coffee.
