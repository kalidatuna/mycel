# Grinder Notes

Hand grinder, conical burrs. Settings are clicks from zero.

- Espresso: 9–11 clicks
- Aeropress: 16
- V60: 22–24
- French press: 30

Burrs matter more than anything else on the bar. A good grinder with a cheap brewer beats the reverse.

Clean it every month or so: rice-based cleaning tablets, then grind 10 g of old beans.
