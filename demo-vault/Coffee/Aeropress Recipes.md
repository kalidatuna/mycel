# Aeropress Recipes

## Inverted, strong
- 17 g, medium-fine
- 200 g water at 85 °C
- Stir 10 times, steep 1:30, flip, press 30 s

## Standard, quick
- 15 g, fine
- 250 g water at 90 °C
- Steep 2 min, swirl, press

Good for travel. Plastic is ugly but it survives anything.
