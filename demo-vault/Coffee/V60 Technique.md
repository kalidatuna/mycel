# V60 Technique

After months of playing with it, my best V60 cups come at a 1:13 ratio. 1:16 is too weak: the cup tastes thin and watery and the sweetness never shows up. A stronger ratio with a slightly coarser grind is the way.

## Recipe
- 18 g coffee, 234 g water at 94 °C
- Bloom with 40 g, swirl, wait 40 s
- Pour to 140 g in slow circles
- Pour to 234 g, gentle swirl
- Total time about 2:45

Rinse the paper first — it really does taste like cardboard otherwise.
