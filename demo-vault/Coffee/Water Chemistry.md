# Water Chemistry

Water is ~98% of a cup of coffee, so it matters.

- Our tap water is very hard (around 300 ppm). Coffee tastes flat and chalky with it.
- Magnesium pulls out fruity, bright flavours; calcium gives body.
- Bicarbonate (buffer) mutes acidity. Too much and everything tastes dull.
- Target roughly 75–150 ppm total hardness, low buffer.

Tried mixing distilled water with a mineral packet: brighter, cleaner cups, and the strength/ratio balance changed too — the same recipe tasted stronger.
