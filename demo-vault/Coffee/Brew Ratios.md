# Brew Ratios

Coffee to water, by weight.

The sweet spot for pour-over is 1:16 (15 g coffee to 240 g water). Anything stronger than 1:15 tastes muddy and heavy to me, and the floral notes disappear. If a cup is sour, grind finer rather than adding more coffee.

| Method | Ratio |
|---|---|
| Pour-over | 1:16 |
| French press | 1:15 |
| Aeropress | 1:12, diluted after |
| Cold brew | 1:8 concentrate |
| Espresso | 1:2 |

See [[Grinder Notes]] for grind settings.
