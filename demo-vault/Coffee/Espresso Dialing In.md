# Espresso Dialing In

Starting point: 18 g in, 36 g out, 25–30 seconds.

- Sour, thin, fast → grind finer
- Bitter, harsh, slow → grind coarser
- Change one thing at a time
- Light roasts want longer ratios (1:2.5) and higher temperature

Finally got the light Ethiopian tasting like fruit tea. Grind on the [[Grinder Notes]] was 9 clicks.
