# Deep Work

## Main idea (my words)
Focused, distraction-free work on hard problems is rare and valuable. Shallow work (email, chat, meetings) is easy to fill a day with but produces little.

## What I'm trying
- Two 90-minute deep blocks every morning, phone in another room
- Chat closed until 11:00
- Shutdown ritual at the end of the day: write tomorrow's first task
- Schedule every hour of the workday, then adjust when it breaks

## Doubts
Some of it assumes you control your calendar. On-call weeks make this impossible.
