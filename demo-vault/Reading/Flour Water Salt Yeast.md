# Flour Water Salt Yeast

Book on bread baking at home.

## Takeaways (my words)
- Time and temperature are the real ingredients. Same dough, different timing, totally different bread.
- Bake bold: a dark crust has much more flavour than a pale one.
- A Dutch oven at home traps steam the way a professional oven does.
- Measure dough temperature, not just room temperature.
