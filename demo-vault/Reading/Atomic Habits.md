# Atomic Habits

## Ideas I use (my words)
- Make the habit obvious: kanji flashcards open on the phone home screen.
- Make it easy: two-minute version first ("one review" not "30 minutes of study").
- Habit stacking: after I make coffee, I do my flashcards.
- Never miss twice.

Applied to [[Japanese Study Plan]] and running.
