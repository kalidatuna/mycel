# Kanji Mnemonics

Little stories that made kanji stick.

- 休 (rest): a person 亻 leaning against a tree 木.
- 明 (bright): sun 日 and moon 月 together.
- 森 (forest): three trees.
- 好 (like): a woman 女 with a child 子.
- 男 (man): rice field 田 plus strength 力.

Writing them out by hand works better than just reviewing on the phone.
