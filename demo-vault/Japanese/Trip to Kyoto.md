# Trip to Kyoto

## Plan
- Fushimi Inari early in the morning, before the crowds
- Philosopher's Path
- Day trip to Nara
- At least three specialty coffee shops — the pour-over culture there is famous

## Language
Practise ordering and asking directions. Review [[Japanese Study Plan]] the week before.

## Packing
- Running shoes (want to run along the Kamo river)
- Aeropress for the hotel
