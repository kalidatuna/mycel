# Zone 2 Training

Zone 2 = easy aerobic pace where you can still hold a conversation. For me that's roughly 135–148 bpm.

About 80% of weekly running should be easy zone 2 running. Easy miles build the aerobic base: more mitochondria, more capillaries, better fat use. They also let you recover so the hard sessions are actually hard.

The other 20%: intervals, tempo runs, hill repeats.

Hard part: it feels embarrassingly slow at first. My zone 2 pace was 6:45/km in March. By August it was 6:05/km at the same heart rate.
