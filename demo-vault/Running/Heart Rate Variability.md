# Heart Rate Variability

HRV = variation in time between heartbeats. Higher usually means the nervous system is recovered; a sudden drop means stress, illness, poor sleep or too much training.

My watch measures it overnight.
- Baseline around 58 ms.
- Drops to the low 40s after alcohol or a hard interval session.
- Lowest readings ever were in the week of the work incident in May.

Useful as a trend, useless as a single number. If it's down three days in a row, I swap the hard session for an easy aerobic run.
