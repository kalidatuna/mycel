# Shin Splints

Pain along the inside of the left shin since late April.

Probable cause: went from 25 to 40 km per week in two weeks. Classic too much, too soon.

What helped:
- Two weeks of cycling instead of running
- Calf raises and tibialis raises, 3×15 daily
- Coming back with 10% weekly increases at most
- Softer surfaces

Fully gone by early June.
