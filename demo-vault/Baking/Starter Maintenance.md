# Starter Maintenance

## Feeding
- Keep at room temperature, feed once a day at 1:1:1 (starter, flour, water by weight).
- Discard down to 50 g before feeding.
- Half rye, half bread flour.
- Ready when it doubles within 4–6 hours of feeding and smells yeasty rather than like acetone.

## Fridge
Not baking for a week: feed it and put it in the fridge. Take it out two days before baking and feed twice.
