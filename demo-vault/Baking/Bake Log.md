# Bake Log

## 2026-03-15
First loaf at 80% hydration. Dough was soup. Couldn't shape it, spread into a pancake. Tasted great though.

## 2026-03-29
80% again. Slightly better with more stretch-and-folds, still flat.

## 2026-04-12
Dropped to 70%. Night and day. Shaped easily, good oven spring.

## 2026-05-03
72%. Best loaf so far.

## 2026-09-28
Looking back: my note in [[Sourdough Hydration]] saying beginners should start at 80% was wrong. 80% is far too wet for a beginner — start around 68–70% and go up as your shaping improves.
