# Focaccia

The easiest impressive bake.

- 500 g bread flour, 400 g water (80%), 10 g salt, 100 g starter (or 3 g instant yeast)
- Mix, rest, 4 sets of folds over 2 hours
- Overnight in the fridge in an oiled tray
- Dimple with oily fingers, rosemary, flaky salt
- 230 °C for 22 minutes

High hydration works here because you don't need to shape it. That's probably why I thought high hydration was easy for loaves too.
