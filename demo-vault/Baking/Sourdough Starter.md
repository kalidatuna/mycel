# Sourdough Starter

Started from scratch with whole rye and water.

## Feeding
- Keep at room temperature and feed once a day at 1:1:1 (starter : flour : water by weight).
- Discard down to 50 g before each feed.
- Use half rye, half bread flour.
- It's ready to bake with when it doubles within 4–6 hours of feeding and smells yeasty, not like nail polish.

## Fridge
If I'm not baking for a week, feed it and put it in the fridge. Take it out two days before baking and do two feeds.
